## Code of Conduct

This project and its contributors are governed by the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct).

By participating, you are expected to uphold this code. Please report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer at **joe.legen.dev@gmail.com**.

### Standards

- **Be respectful** — Engage in constructive dialogue and respect diverse perspectives.
- **Be inclusive** — Use welcoming and respectful language.
- **Be collaborative** — Work together to maintain and improve the project.
- **Be professional** — Focus on technical merit and community benefit.

### Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are committed to respecting the privacy and security of all reporters.
